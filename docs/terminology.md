# Terminology

How MapForge's words read in each Interface Language. The rule behind it is ADR-0022:
rulebook terms are copied from the official D&D 5.5e edition in that language, MapForge's
own terms are chosen once and recorded here, and the DM's own words are never translated.
`CONTEXT.md` defines what each term *means*; this file only says what it is *called*.

Only terms that appear on screen are listed. A change that first puts a glossary term on
screen adds its row here for every language.

| Language | Code | Rulebook source | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | `en` | — (the source language; British spelling, per ADR-0022) | — |
| Italiano | `it` | SRD 5.2.1 IT (WotC) | **reviewed** — rows marked *proposed* excepted |
| Deutsch | `de` | SRD 5.2.1 DE (WotC) | proposed |
| Français | `fr` | SRD 5.2.1 FR (WotC) | proposed |
| Español | `es` | SRD 5.2.1 ES (WotC; Spain) | proposed |
| Português (Brasil) | `pt-BR` | *Livro do Jogador* (Galápagos 2014, Asmodee 2026) | proposed |
| Ελληνικά | `el` | none exists — rulebook terms stay in English | proposed |

A translation is corrected here first and in the string catalogue second, never the other way
round.

**Capitals.** English capitalises glossary terms mid-sentence ("put back into the Order");
that is our glossary's habit, not the language's. Each translation follows its own book
instead: Italian, Spanish and Portuguese use sentence case ("rimetti nell'ordine"), French
capitalises rules terms as the 2024 edition does (*action Bonus*, *Réaction*), German
capitalises every noun anyway, Greek uses sentence case.

## Rulebook terms

Copied verbatim; not ours to improve. Where the 2014 and 2024 translations differ, the 2024
one is used. Greek has no column: its rulebook terms are the English ones.

Sources: WotC's translated SRD 5.2.1 for `it`, `de`, `fr`, `es`
(<https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd>); for `pt-BR`, the 2014 Galápagos books and reference sites
citing the 2026 Asmodee *Livro do Jogador*, since no Portuguese SRD exists. Rows marked †
could not be checked against the 2024 Portuguese book.

### Distances

Every edition above is metric at **1,5 m** per square, with a decimal comma. French writes a
fractional distance with two decimals — **1,50 m**, **4,50 m**, **7,50 m** — and whole ones
bare (**9 m**); the others write **1,5 m**. Greek follows the European editions: **1,5 m**.

## MapForge's own terms

No book has these, so each is a choice. *Rejected* lists what was turned down and why, the way
`_Avoid_` does in `CONTEXT.md`; the same two traps recur in every language:

- **Never reuse an official rulebook word.** Big is not the official Large, Pawn Kind is not
  the rulebook's creature *type*, Change is not the word for Modifier.
- **Never collide with a class name.** German *Kämpfer* is the Fighter.
